bre is a two-person operation in batumi, georgia. we look after the apartments, write the guide, host the guests, and run the business with our own hands. since 2022.

— our home for the last four years
— how it started

we arrived for a winter.
we stayed.

we came to batumi in early 2022, thinking we'd give it a season. we'd both spent the last decade working in moscow — one of us in property and brand operations, the other running a plant studio. the plan was to rent a flat for three months, see the sea, drink some wine, decide what's next.

three months turned into six. six turned into a year. by the end of that year we'd bought our first flat — half because we'd fallen for the city, half because the maths just worked. we renovated it ourselves, listed it, hosted our first guests over a long, slow winter.

then a friend asked us to manage hers. then her friend asked. then the owner of the flat below. by the end of 2023 we were running ten apartments, writing a city guide on the side, and quietly turning what was supposed to be a winter break into a real business.

we're still here. still surprised.

— how we work

five things
we hold to.

01

we use what we recommend.

every flat we manage, we'd stay in. every café we list, we drink at. every renovation contractor we suggest, we've worked with on our own walls.

02

small on purpose.

ten apartments, not a hundred. one city, not five. we know every door, every neighbour, every quirk. we'd rather grow slowly than badly.

03

transparent about numbers.

30/70 split, no hidden fees, no "extras". monthly report with every line, every receipt. you see what we see.

04

we write what we mean.

no marketing fluff. no "unique experience". no "best in town". if a place is good, we say why. if it's not, we don't list it.

05

we answer ourselves.

no chatbots, no support tickets, no "the team will get back to you". the message lands with one of us, day or night.

06

we stay.

batumi is home, not a project. we built our lives here, and we'll be here when you arrive.
