— what we actually do

everything except
opening the door.

six things, every month. listed plainly so you know what your 30% pays for.
01

listing & photography

we photograph your flat in real light, write descriptions in two languages, and publish on 3—4 platforms (airbnb, booking, our own site, telegram channels). no stock photos, no fake adjectives.

02

dynamic pricing

batumi prices swing. we adjust nightly rates based on season, competitor pricing, and event calendar — so the flat doesn't sit empty in shoulder months and doesn't undersell in peak.

03

guests, in 5 languages

russian, english, turkish, hebrew, georgian. we reply within minutes, day and night. fast replies move your listing up — and keep guests calm.

04

the little things

soap, shampoo, conditioner, toothbrushes, slippers, laundry pods. tea, coffee, spices, olive oil. a printed local guide in every flat. small things, but guests notice.

05

cleaning, laundry, fixes

after every guest. plus the small stuff that breaks — lightbulbs, loose handles, dripping taps. plumbers and electricians when needed — coordinated, never bothering you.

06

monthly report

at the end of every month, you get a transparent breakdown: bookings, revenue, our cut, your share, operating costs. plus calendar access — see your bookings in real time.
