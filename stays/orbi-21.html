— about this flat

a high floor, a long balcony, and the sea right in front.

A 1-bedroom on the 21st floor of Orbi Sea Towers, with full open sea view from every window. The bedroom is separate, the bath is marble, the kitchen is small but properly equipped — coffee maker, induction stove, dishwasher. Workspace by the window. The balcony is the main reason to book this one — sunsets are nuts.

Walking distance to the central beach, 8 minutes on foot. Old Boulevard is 12 minutes. The building has its own pool, gym and a coffee shop downstairs. Good for couples, solo travelers, or anyone who values quiet at altitude.

— frequently asked

questions you might have.

How do I check in?

We meet you at the building. Direct bookings: you'll have our Telegram, we coordinate arrival time. Airbnb/Booking: same — message us through the platform after booking. The building has 24/7 concierge but the keys come from us.

Is parking available?

Not included with this flat. The building has a paid underground lot (₾10/day, ~$3.50). Street parking on side streets is usually possible but not guaranteed. We can help arrange airport transfer if you prefer not to drive.

What's nearby for food and groceries?

Two supermarkets within 5 minutes (Carrefour and Spar). The full restaurant list is in our eat & drink section. Pure Bistro for oysters, Nova Coffee for mornings, Japan 33 for sunsets.

Can I pay in installments for a long stay?

Yes — for stays over 14 nights we split into 2 payments. Over 30 nights, monthly. We use bank transfer or Wise. Crypto is fine too. No card processing for direct bookings (yet).

Why book direct vs Airbnb?

Same flat, same keys, same us. Direct = no platform fees → you save ~10%. Airbnb = built-in protection if you've never stayed with us before. Both work. Pick what feels right.

What about taxes and city fees?

Georgia has no tourist tax for short-term rentals. The price you see is what you pay (plus cleaning fee for stays under 7 nights — $25 one-time).
