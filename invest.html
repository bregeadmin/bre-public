— step 01 / find

what we know
about the market.

no commission from sellers. we work for you, not for the building. consult-only on this step if you prefer.

batumi has roughly three layers of property — old soviet stock in the centre, mid-range new builds across the boulevard and chavchavadze, and the towers (orbi, panorama, arena) that dominate the skyline. each plays differently for short-term rental.

we walk you through what's worth buying, where, at what price — and what to avoid: the buildings with weak management, the developers with delayed handovers, the districts that look great in renderings but don't rent in real life.

we also help with the boring-but-critical stuff: contracts, cadastral checks, residency questions, the tax setup. georgian property law is friendlier than you'd think — but only if you know the moves.

avg. yield ~11% net annual yield across our managed portfolio. updated quarterly.
income tax 5% flat tax on rental income in georgia. setup takes 1 day.
visa-free 1 year for most countries. property ownership doesn't require residency.
our experience on-the-ground we already rent and operate across six districts. we know where to buy because we live and earn here.

first guest.

fixed price agreed upfront, no surprises. weekly photo + video report. about 60—90 days for a typical 50m² flat.

most buyers in batumi receive their flat as a black or white frame — walls, windows, plumbing rough-in, and not much else. you can't move in, you can't rent it. you need a renovation that's built for rental, not for personal taste.

we do turnkey — design, materials, contractors, furniture, appliances, textiles, art on the walls. one team, one timeline, one price. you get weekly photo updates and a finished flat with the keys in our hand, ready for guests.

the number on the right is all-in: design, work, materials, furniture, full equipment, decor. nothing extra to buy. nothing forgotten. ready for a guest the day we hand it over.

three levels available — economy, comfort, luxury. the figure shown is a typical mid-range comfort tier. we'll walk through which level fits your goal at the first call.
