— privacy policy

privacy, in plain words.

— updated may 2026 · jurisdiction georgia

We're a small operator. We collect only what we need to host you and answer your questions. We don't sell your data to anyone. If you write us — we'll write back; if you stop writing — we forget about you.

what we collect

When you contact us through the website, Telegram, WhatsApp, email or phone, we keep the basics needed to reply:

  • your name (or whatever you call yourself)
  • your contact method (email, phone, telegram handle)
  • the message itself
  • any details you share about your stay or property

what we do with it

We use this to reply to you, manage your booking or contract, send updates about your stay or property, and improve our service. That's it. No marketing automation, no resale, no ad targeting.

who else sees it

For bookings, we may share your name and arrival dates with the building concierge or cleaning team. Some data may pass through Airbnb, Booking.com or Suточно if you booked via their platforms — their privacy rules apply on top of ours.

We don't share data with advertisers, data brokers or analytics resellers.

how long we keep it

Active correspondence and bookings: as long as needed plus 1 year for accounting. Old conversations: we may delete them or you can ask us to.

your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • show what data we have about you
  • correct it
  • delete it (within what Georgian tax law lets us delete)
  • stop using it for anything beyond the basics

Just write us. We'll handle it within a few days.

cookies & analytics

The website uses minimal cookies for language preference and to remember if you've seen the dot fly. No third-party tracking, no Facebook Pixel, no Google Analytics user-ID tracking. We may add basic anonymous analytics later to understand which pages people read — but never to identify individuals.

changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date above and mention it to anyone who's in active conversation with us.

Questions about privacy? Write us at hello@bre.ge or in Telegram. We answer ourselves.
